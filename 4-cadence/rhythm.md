# My rhythm

The habits that keep your AI OS sharp, plus what runs on its own. Claude reminds you when one is due.

## Every day
- Open this folder first when something new lands. Ask before you dig.
- Saving is automatic. Type `/save` if you want a written wrap up of what you did and decided.

## Every week
- `/level-up`: pick one task from `3-capabilities/tasks-to-automate.md` and move it one stage closer to running on its own.
- `/grill-me` on anything new that came up.

## Every month
- `/audit`: your report card across the Four Cs. Reports save in `4-cadence/audits/`.
- Check `2-connections/connections.md`: anything new to connect?

## Every quarter
- Fill in next quarter's file in `1-context/priorities/` and close out this one.
- Look back at `1-context/decisions.md`: what worked, what to stop.

See `schedules.md`.
